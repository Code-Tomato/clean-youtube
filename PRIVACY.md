# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

Clean YouTube does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

## What the extension does

Clean YouTube runs only on youtube.com and m.youtube.com. It adds styles to those pages to hide the parts you choose (such as the home feed, Shorts, or recommendations) and, if you ask it to, switches YouTube's autoplay toggle off. It does not read, record, or send anywhere what you watch, search for, or type.

## What is stored, and where

- **Your settings** (which options are on or off) are saved with your browser's extension storage (`storage.sync`). If you have browser sync turned on, your browser may sync them between your own devices. The developer never receives them.
- **One flag on youtube.com** (`cy-autoplay-off` in the page's local storage) remembers that the extension turned autoplay off, so it can turn it back on if you disable the option. It never leaves your device.

## Network requests

The extension makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics, tracking, ads, or remote code.
